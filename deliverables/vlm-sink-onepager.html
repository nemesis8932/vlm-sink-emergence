When Do Attention Sinks Emerge in VLM Pretraining — and Do Their Signatures Decouple?

From-scratch attention-sink emergence study on a 222M vision-language model · pipeline-validation + early-window stage · single-author, seeking mentor / corresponding author

The question

Trained LLMs develop an attention sink: one token (usually the first) absorbs a large share of attention. In practice this co-occurs with two representation-level signatures at that token — massive activations (a handful of hidden dimensions with outsized norm) and value-norm drain. The literature treats the three as one phenomenon and studies it at inference, on frozen models. Two things are unmeasured: (1) when/where these form during multimodal pretraining, and (2) whether attention-concentration and the norm/representation signatures actually move together — or decouple. The first token here is a SigLIP image patch, not a BOS token, which makes the VLM setting a clean natural experiment.

Setup

nanoVLM-222M, trained from scratch: a bidirectional vision encoder (SigLIP-B/16) whose patch embeddings are projected into the prefix of an autoregressive decoder LM (SmolLM2-135M architecture, 30 causal-attention layers). The attention sinks we study live in that decoder's causal self-attention — position 0 is the first image-patch token in its sequence — so this is fundamentally a decoder phenomenon, with the encoder supplying the visual prefix. Dense probing every 100 steps via an eager fp32 re-walk validated against the live forward (rel-err < 1e-2). Three signatures logged separately per (layer,head): concentration Sinkε, value-norm ratio, hidden-norm ratio. Four arms each isolate one lever (softmax baseline · Qiu σ-gate · sigmoid-without-softmax · text-pretrained init).

Fig 1 — Decoupling in the 1B-token from-scratch baseline. The concentration sink never fires (Sink0.3 = 0 the entire run) while the massive-activation ratio climbs +130% (1.43→3.22). Concentration and massive activation are dissociated within a single run. Dashed line = an OOM-driven weights-only restart, audited not to affect the signature trajectory.
Fig 2 — Four-way dissociation. Holding data, optimizer and architecture fixed, each lever yields a different combination of the three signatures — e.g. sigmoid gives strong concentration with no massive activation; text-init inherits all three at step 0, relocated onto the first image token. They are independently controllable, not facets of one thing.

Why this should interest you (embeddings)

Massive activations are the same outlier / "rogue" dimensions that dominate embedding geometry and distort cosine similarity. The result here says attention-concentration and these outlier-dimension signatures can be dialed independently — i.e. a training-time lever on representation geometry, not merely on where attention points. Whether that identification (massive activation ≡ embedding outlier dimension) is the right bridge is exactly one place I want your read.
